Annotated bibliography

Five sources used throughout this tutorial

[1] Funk et al., 2018 — PressNET

Full citation: Christopher Funk, Savinay Nagendra, Jesse Scott, Bharadwaj Ravichandran, John H. Challis, Robert T. Collins, and Yanxi Liu. 2018. Learning Dynamics from Kinematics: Estimating 2D Foot Pressure Maps from Video Frames. arXiv:1811.12607.

Synopsis: Introduces PressNET, a convolutional network that predicts a foot-pressure heatmap from a single frame of 2D body pose. Trains on six people performing Tai Chi, with synchronized video and insole pressure sensors as ground truth. Beats a K-nearest-neighbor baseline on pressure error and on center-of-pressure error, and shows its predictions stay smooth over time. This is the main source for the whole tutorial.

Peer-reviewed adjacentPrimary source

Reliability: High. Detailed methodology, real experimental data, and a named research group at Penn State, though the version used is an arXiv preprint rather than a published journal article.

[2] Scott et al., 2020 — kinematics to dynamics

Full citation: Jesse Scott, Christopher Funk, Bharadwaj Ravichandran, John H. Challis, Robert T. Collins, and Yanxi Liu. 2020. From Kinematics to Dynamics: Estimating Center of Pressure and Base of Support from Video Frames of Human Motion. arXiv:2001.00657.

Synopsis: A follow-up study from the same research group. Adds a larger dataset, a simpler fully connected network alongside PressNET, and extends the output from pressure alone to center of pressure and base of support. Used throughout this tutorial to confirm PressNET's results generalize and to introduce base of support.

Reliability: High. Same authors and lab as [1], larger dataset, and consistent methodology, though also an arXiv preprint.
